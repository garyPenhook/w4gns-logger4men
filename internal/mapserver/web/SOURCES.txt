World polygons: Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 countries, v5.1.2.
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Conversion: flatten Polygon/MultiPolygon features into polygon arrays,
retain exterior and interior rings, round longitude/latitude to 2 decimals,
discard feature properties. world.json contains these coordinate arrays.
Country boundaries are cartographic context, not a statement of sovereignty.

US state polygons: Natural Earth 1:110m admin-1 states/provinces, v5.1.2,

Same conversion as world.json (flatten to polygon arrays, retain exterior
and interior rings, round to 2 decimals, discard feature properties).
